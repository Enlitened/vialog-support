Privacy

Vialog is a personal record kept on your iPhone. This page says what it keeps, where that stays, and what leaves the device. Last changed 7 September 2026.

Vialog does not collect any data about you.

What the app keeps

The routines, schedules, quantities, injection sites, records, check-ins, weights, notes and the amount on hand that you enter. Small settings such as the schedule zone, the appearance and whether App Lock is on. Nothing is collected that you did not type or choose.

Where it stays

On this device, in the app's own storage, protected by the device passcode. There is no account, no sign-in and no server. The developer cannot see your records and has no way to ask for them. If iCloud Backup or a computer backup is on for this iPhone, the app's data is included in that backup under Apple's terms, encrypted the way the rest of the device backup is.

What leaves the device

Only what you send. A CSV export, a PDF report or a backup file is created when you ask for one and handed to the share sheet; where it goes from there is your choice, and anyone who has the file can read it. The app makes no network connection of its own.

Analytics and tracking

None. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no third-party code that phones home. It does not track you across other apps or websites.

Reminders

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device; no reminder passes through a server. By default a reminder says only that you have a scheduled entry. Settings can add the routine's name and quantity, which then appear on the lock screen for anyone who can see the phone.

App Lock

When on, the app asks for Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode before it opens, and shows a blank cover in the app switcher. Authentication is done by iOS; the app never sees your face, fingerprint or passcode.

The purchase

Vialog Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles the payment and keeps the receipt; the app only learns whether the purchase is owned. The app does not receive your name, email address or payment details.

Your control

Everything can be exported as CSV or backed up as a file at any time, without a purchase. Delete all records in Settings removes every record from the device, and removing the app removes its storage. There is nothing held elsewhere to ask for or to delete.

Children

The app is not directed at children and keeps no record of anyone's age.

Changes

If a future version changes any of this, the change appears on this page, on the app's own Privacy screen, and in the version's release notes before it takes effect.
